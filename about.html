Our Story

QuickFix SA was founded in 2018 by a group of passionate tradesmen who were tired of seeing homeowners get ripped off by unreliable contractors. We started with just one bakkie, a toolbox, and a promise: to always show up on time and do the job right.

Over the years, we have grown into a trusted maintenance provider across Gauteng. Our founder realized that whether you are a tenant dealing with a leaking geyser or a landlord preparing a property for rent, you just want someone reliable to fix the problem without the drama.

Today, QuickFix SA handles hundreds of repairs every month, but our core philosophy remains the same: honest work at a fair price.

Mission & Vision

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide quotes before starting?

Yes, absolutely. We assess the job first and give you a clear, written quote before any work begins. There are no hidden costs.

Are your handymen insured and vetted?

Yes. All our workers undergo background checks and are fully insured for public liability. You are completely protected when we are on your property.

Which areas do you service?

We service the greater Johannesburg and Pretoria areas, including Sandton, Randburg, Centurion, Midrand, Fourways, and surrounding suburbs.

Do you bring your own materials?

Yes, we can supply all necessary materials (pipes, paint, electrical components) and add them to the final invoice at cost. Alternatively, if you have already purchased the materials, we are happy to use those.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept EFT (Electronic Funds Transfer), cash, and major credit/debit cards. Payment is due within 7 days of the invoice date.

Do you offer a warranty on your work?

Yes, we offer a 3-month workmanship warranty on all general repairs. If something we fixed breaks again within that time, we will come back and fix it for free.
